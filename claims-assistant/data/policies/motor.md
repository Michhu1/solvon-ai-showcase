# Motor Own Damage Insurance — General Terms (synthetic)

Synthetic document written for this demo. It is not a real insurance product.

### MOT-1.1 Scope of cover
The policy covers the vehicle named in the policy schedule against accidental damage, loss and theft
during the period of insurance, within the territory of Europe.

### MOT-2.1 Collision damage
We pay for damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle, a person, an animal
or a fixed object, including damage caused while parking or reversing. A deductible of 500 PLN applies to
every claim. The sum insured is the market value of the vehicle, at most 80,000 PLN.

### MOT-2.2 Theft of the vehicle
We pay for the theft of the whole vehicle if it was locked and the alarm or immobiliser was active.
The theft must be reported to the police within 48 hours and the police report sent to us. No deductible
applies. The sum insured is the market value of the vehicle, at most 80,000 PLN.

### MOT-2.3 Glass damage
We pay for the repair or replacement of the windscreen, side windows and rear window, including damage
caused by stones thrown up by other vehicles. No deductible applies. The limit is 3,000 PLN per claim.

### MOT-2.4 Natural forces
We pay for damage caused by hail, flood, storm, lightning or a falling tree or branch. A deductible of
500 PLN applies. The sum insured is the market value of the vehicle, at most 80,000 PLN.

### MOT-3.1 Exclusions
We do not pay for damage that occurred while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs,
drove without a valid driving licence, or took part in a race or speed test.

### MOT-4.1 Reporting a claim
Report the damage within 7 days. Send photos of the damage and a repair estimate. For claims above
5,000 PLN an adjuster inspects the vehicle before repair.
