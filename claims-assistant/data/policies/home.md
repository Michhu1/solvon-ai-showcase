# Home Insurance — General Terms (synthetic)

Synthetic document written for this demo. It is not a real insurance product.

### HOM-1.1 Scope of cover
The policy covers the building or apartment at the address in the policy schedule and the movable
property inside it (furniture, electronics, clothing) against the events listed in section 2.

### HOM-2.1 Fire and explosion
We pay for damage caused by fire, smoke, explosion or lightning strike. A deductible of 300 PLN applies.
The limit is 400,000 PLN for the building and 100,000 PLN for movable property.

### HOM-2.2 Water damage
We pay for damage caused by a sudden escape of water: a burst pipe, a failed appliance hose, or water
leaking from a neighbouring apartment. A deductible of 300 PLN applies. The limit is 50,000 PLN.

### HOM-2.3 Burglary
We pay for movable property stolen after a forced entry into the home, and for repairing doors, locks
and windows damaged during the break-in. The burglary must be reported to the police. A deductible of
300 PLN applies. The limit is 30,000 PLN.

### HOM-2.4 Flood and storm
We pay for damage caused by a flood from rivers or heavy rain, or by storm winds above 17 m/s.
A deductible of 500 PLN applies. The limit is 100,000 PLN.

### HOM-3.1 Exclusions
We do not pay for damage caused by gradual leakage or long-term damp and mould, normal wear and tear,
or theft without signs of forced entry.

### HOM-4.1 Reporting a claim
Report the damage within 3 days. Send photos and a list of damaged items with their value.
Claims above 5,000 PLN and every burglary are handled by an adjuster.
