# Travel Insurance — General Terms (synthetic)

Synthetic document written for this demo. It is not a real insurance product.

### TRV-1.1 Scope of cover
The policy covers the insured person during a trip outside their country of residence, for the dates
shown in the policy schedule.

### TRV-2.1 Medical expenses abroad
We pay for necessary medical treatment abroad after a sudden illness or accident: doctor visits,
hospital stays, medicines and medical transport. No deductible applies. The limit is 200,000 PLN.
Send the medical documentation and the bills.

### TRV-2.2 Baggage
We pay for baggage lost or damaged by the carrier, or stolen during the trip. A deductible of 100 PLN
applies. The limit is 3,000 PLN. Loss by an airline must be confirmed by a property irregularity report
from the carrier.

### TRV-2.3 Trip cancellation
We pay for the non-refundable cost of a trip cancelled because of a sudden illness or accident of the
insured person, confirmed by a doctor. No deductible applies. The limit is 10,000 PLN.

### TRV-3.1 Exclusions
We do not pay for treatment of conditions that existed before the trip (pre-existing conditions),
injuries suffered under the influence of alcohol, or extreme sports unless the policy was extended.

### TRV-4.1 Reporting a claim
Report the claim within 14 days after returning. Medical claims above 5,000 PLN are reviewed by a
medical adjuster.
